FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about booking, hardware, cancellation, and cafe perks.

How does seat booking work?

You can choose your desired date, start time slot, duration, and specific PC or console seat on our interactive 2D arena seat map. Your chosen seat is held exclusively for you during checkout, and upon payment confirmation you receive an instant digital QR ticket.

Can I bring my own peripherals (Mouse, Keyboard, Headset)?

Absolutely! While our rigs are equipped with tournament-grade Razer and Logitech G Pro gear, players are welcome to plug in their own mice, mechanical keyboards, audio DACs, or console controllers via front USB 3.2 hubs.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

You can cancel or reschedule any booking up to 1 hour before the scheduled session start time directly through your Customer Dashboard. Refunds are credited back to your original payment method or wallet instantly.

How do loyalty XP and reward points work?

Every booking earns you XP reward points (10 XP per ₹100 spent). You also earn 100 XP upon sign up and 200 XP for every friend who signs up using your unique referral code. Points can be redeemed for discounts, free extra hours, and cafe refreshments.
