VENUE GUIDELINES

ARENA RULES & POLICIES

To ensure an elite, high-standard esports environment, all players are expected to respect the following standards.

1. ZERO TOLERANCE FOR CHEATING

Use of third-party aimbots, wallhacks, memory injectors, or illicit game modifications is strictly forbidden and results in immediate ejection, account forfeiture, and tournament blacklist.

2. HARDWARE CARE & RESPECT

Do not smash keyboards, drop headsets, or abuse peripherals in moments of tilt. Any intentional physical damage will be billed directly to the customer.

3. FOOD & DRINK RESTRICTIONS

Only spill-proof beverages with lids are permitted in proximity to keyboards. Greasy snacks must be consumed at the designated lounge area.

4. SPORTSMANSHIP & TOXICITY

Gammers Adda is a home for all gamers. Verbal harassment, hate speech, or physical intimidation will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
